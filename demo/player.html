Duka Books: the books a corner shop never kept.

Kenya's small shops run on an exercise book and M-Pesa SMS. With no records, the only credit is Fuliza, the overdraft. Duka Books keeps double-entry books from what the owner already sends on WhatsApp.

Built during the event · Oct 27 – Nov 4, 2026

Confirmed books, in Swahili, in the chat they already use.

  • Muse Glimmer 30B (Meta open weights) reads ledger pages and receipts
  • SAM 3 splits a pile of receipts into one entry each
  • Llama 3.1 8B reads typed notes, fixes and answers questions
  • Every entry posts to a double-entry journal; P&L built from it
