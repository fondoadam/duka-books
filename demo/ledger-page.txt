DUKA BOOKS DEMO: WHAT TO WRITE IN THE EXERCISE BOOK
====================================================

Write this by hand on one ruled page, in blue or black pen, the way a shop owner would.
Keep it natural, but make every number readable, except line 2 (see the note below).


THE PAGE (copy everything between the lines)
--------------------------------------------

                30/10

1.  Unga 2kg  x3              540
2.  Mchele 3kg                450      <- write the 4 a bit messy, so it could pass for a 9
3.  Mkate  x2                 120
4.  Maziwa  x3                180
5.  Sabuni                     95
6.  Mama Njeri (deni) mafuta  200

                    Jumla   1,585

--------------------------------------------

Why it looks like this:
- Line 2 is the one the video "fixes" by sending "2 = 450". Its true amount is 450.
- Line 6 is a credit sale ("deni" = owed), so the bot books it as a debt, not cash.
- "Jumla" is the total. The bot checks that the lines add up to it: 540 + 450 + 120 + 180 + 95 + 200 = 1,585.
- No phone numbers or full customer names, because the page will be in a public video.


TAKING THE PHOTO
----------------
- Daylight, straight down from above, no shadow from your phone.
