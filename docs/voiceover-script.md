## Recording it

1. Use your phone's voice recorder in a quiet room, with soft furnishings around you if you can.
2. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to one side.
3. Say a test line, then play it back to check it's clear.
4. Read the whole script in one take, without watching the video. Speak slowly, like you're explaining it to a friend.
5. Pause for two seconds between lines. If you stumble, stop, pause, and say that line again.
6. Send the original audio file to the Mac mini.

Your natural accent and pace are fine. Don't rush to fit the times: I'll place each line against the video and trim the
pauses.
