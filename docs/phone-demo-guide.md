# Phone demo recording guide

A 40 to 60 second screen recording of the real bot on your phone. It proves the demo works live.

## Before you record

1. **Save the number as a contact** called `Duka Books`: +1 555 162 9147. Otherwise the chat shows a bare number.
2. **Clear your test books.** In the bot chat, send `futa data yangu`, then tap **Ndiyo, futa**. This removes the
   sample ledger and the M-Pesa test for good, so your numbers start clean. Only do this once.
3. **Turn on Do Not Disturb**, so no notifications appear on screen.
4. **Leave the Mac mini awake.** Text replies run on it.

## Write the page

Use your real exercise book and pen. **Write the date you're recording**, large and well inside the page: a small date
at the very top edge can be misread. The bot's week starts on Monday, so an old date can fall outside "wiki hii". If
the date isn't today, the bot shows it as 📅 Tarehe before you confirm.

Keep to about 6 lines, with clear headings. Use made-up customer names only. Make sales bigger than stock, because stock
you buy counts against profit straight away.

```
1/11/26

NIMENUNUA STOCK
Unga bale 1          1,400
Sukari 2kg             380

MAUZO
Mkate x10              650
Maziwa x8              480
Sabuni x5              550
Mama Wanjiru deni      300
Unga 2kg x6          1,080
Jumla mauzo          3,060
```

Check your total adds up. The bot compares it with the lines it reads, so a wrong total shows a warning.

Photograph it from straight above, in daylight, with the whole page in frame and no shadow. Take the photo before you
start recording.

## Start the screen recording

- **iPhone:** Settings, Control Center, add Screen Recording. Swipe down from the top right and tap the record button.
- **Android:** swipe down twice from the top and tap Screen Record.

Leave sound off, and hold the phone upright.

## Record these steps

Take one calm take, and leave a pause between steps. Don't worry about waiting: I'll trim the gaps.

1. Open the Duka Books chat. Send the photo of the page, with no caption.
2. Wait for **Nimepata rekodi…**. It can take a minute or more, so keep recording.
3. Scroll slowly through the lines so they can be read.
4. If a line is wrong, tap **Si sahihi** and send the fix, such as `2 = 450`. That makes good footage, so keep it. If
   all lines are right, skip this.
5. Tap **Sawa, andika** and wait for the ✅.
6. Send `faida wiki hii?` and wait for the answer.
7. Send `ripoti ya mwezi`, wait for the PDF, then tap to open it for about 3 seconds.
8. Stop recording.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Samahani, kuna tatizo" after the photo:** the free photo quota has probably run out. Don't keep retrying, as that
  can't help and may spend more of it. Tell me, and wait for it to reset, which can take up to 24 hours.
- **Text gets no reply:** tell me. The Mac mini or its tunnel may need a look.
