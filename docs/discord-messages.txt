👋 Hi all, I'm Adam, a solo full-stack developer from Nairobi, Kenya, in the Indie track. Really grateful to be part of this hackathon, and thanks to the organisers and Meta for putting it together!

I'm building **Duka Books**, a WhatsApp bookkeeper for Kenya's small shops ("dukas"). The owner sends a photo of their handwritten ledger, receipts, M-Pesa messages or a Swahili voice note. Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 turn those into proper books, and the owner confirms each entry with one tap. At month end they get a profit-and-loss statement they can take to a lender.

Stack: Convex, the WhatsApp Cloud API and the Meta Model API. By day I build BizStudio, a business app for Kenyan SMEs.

Happy to swap notes with anyone on WhatsApp bots, vision extraction from messy handwriting, or Swahili speech. I'd love to stay in touch after the hackathon too, so let's connect:
